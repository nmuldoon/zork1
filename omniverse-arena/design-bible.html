00 · Map blueprint

Four worlds, one 8 × 8 km map

Each 4 × 4 km quadrant is a separate digital world with its own hazard, way of moving and central landmark. Fault Lines run where the worlds collide, and the Nexus sits at the centre.

NW · Close range

Neon Midway

  • Look: 80s arcade the size of a city. Patterned carpet streets, cabinet towers, a synthwave sunset that never sets
  • Hazard, Ghost Sweep: giant ghosts patrol the maze streets. Grab a Power Pellet to turn the tables for 10s
  • Traversal: driveable synthwave cars on a neon highway loop, with boost pads
  • POI, The Cabinet: a skyscraper-sized arcade machine. Each floor is a playable game screen. The Copper Key race starts here
NE · Mid range, vertical

Arcanum Reach

  • Look: floating islands, crystal forests, castles linked by rune bridges
  • Hazard, Mana Tide: waves of wild magic drop gravity low for 30s. Spells cost half, guns drift off-target
  • Traversal: rideable griffins from roost points, plus wind currents between islands. Mounts can be shot down
  • POI, Spire of Ascension: a giant parkour tower. Home of the Jade Key
SE · Close quarters, vertical

Neo-Kowloon Grid

  • Look: rain-soaked stacked megacity, hologram ads, alleys ten storeys deep
  • Hazard, Data Corruption Storm: roaming storm cells scramble the HUD, kill the minimap and turn drones hostile
  • Traversal: grind rails along maglev lines, grapple hooks and wall-running
  • POI, The Mainframe: a server tower. Capture its uplink to ping every enemy in the sector for 15s
SW · Long range, open

The Bitlands

  • Look: flat 8-bit plains, block mountains, a four-colour sky
  • Hazard, Block Fall: giant falling block shapes stack up as new cover. A completed row clears and deletes anyone standing on it
  • Traversal: warp tubes between hills, bounce pads and a double jump that only works here
  • POI, 8-Bit Citadel: a sprite castle that rebuilds itself every phase. The Crystal Key hunt starts here
Fault Lines

The seams between sectors are 200m blended strips: pixel blocks sticking out of castle walls, neon signs on dragon bones. They're the main crossing routes and the best ambush spots.

The Nexus

The centre point where all four worlds meet. The Final Gate stands here and the last Merge Zone always ends here.

Range mix

Each sector favours a different fight distance, so every loadout has a home: Midway and the Grid for close range, Arcanum for mid, the Bitlands for long.

01 · Cross-genre balance

Lasers vs magic vs steel

Every weapon shares one set of hidden stats: damage per second, range, time-to-kill and one resource bar called Charge. Theme changes the look, never the power.

ArchetypeStrengthCountered by
TechLong range, accurateShields soak beams; EMP spells disable gear
ArcaneArea damage, curses, utilityLong cast times; interrupted by melee rush
SteelHigh burst, parries projectilesKited at range before it closes the gap
PixelFast, small, hard to readLow HP; one-shot by area damage
Sector Physics

Home turf matters

Weapons get +15% in their home sector and −15% in the opposite one. Magic glitches in Cyberpunk. Lasers fizzle in Fantasy.

Melee fairness

Swords can win

Timed parries deflect projectiles. Every melee weapon has a gap-closer. Melee hits deal the highest damage in the game.

Loadouts

Mix any theme

Carry 2–3 weapons from any sector and swap to suit the ground you're fighting on.

Three ways to drop in

Solo

  • Live player count on each portal
  • Late entry: random spawn plus a loot bonus
Classic

DeLorean Drop

  • A flying DeLorean, dragon or Galaga ship crosses the map
  • Fantasy: glide on wings
  • Pixel: slow 8-bit chute
  • Cyberpunk: jetpack boosts
Squad

Respawn Cartridge

  • Squad loads as one cartridge into a giant cabinet
  • Pick a level-select tile on the map grid
  • Each tile shows a loot rarity rating
03 · The closing zone

The Derez Wave

The map edge is Null Code: the server un-rendering itself to save processing power. Whole sectors get deleted one at a time, with a 60-second warning.

Phase 1

Lag Zone

Frame-skip, input delay, slow damage.

Phase 2

Wireframe

Textures vanish, minimap fails, damage rises.

Phase 3

Null

Fast HP drain. "Avatar deletion imminent."

Merge Zone

The final circle fuses the last surviving sectors: a neon city covered in dragon fire and pixel blocks.

Glitch Rifts

Short-lived safe pockets inside the Null that hold rare loot. High risk, high reward.

Legendary · Pixel + Modern

Retro-Gun

  • Assault rifle that fires 8-bit pixel bullets
  • Bullets bounce off walls at right angles, like an arcade shooter
  • Targets it kills burst into pixels
  • Catch: slow, visible bullets that are easy to dodge
Mythic · Robot + Fairy

Robotic-Fairy Wings

  • Chrome wings that leave a trail of fairy dust
  • Hover, glide and a 5s flight burst
  • The dust heals squadmates who fly through it
  • Catch: overheats, and the dust trail shows where you've been
Epic · Fantasy + Sci-fi

Plasma Excalibur

  • Legendary-style sword with a plasma edge
  • Every successful parry stores a charge
  • Release 3 charges as a plasma wave
  • Catch: the charge is lost if you swap weapons

Spell Cartridge Gauntlet

  • Robot fist with a cartridge slot
  • Slot in spell cartridges found as loot to cast fireball, frost or blink
  • Blow on the cartridge to reload

1-Up Amulet

  • An 8-bit heart set in an elven amulet
  • Revives you once, at full health, with a chiptune jingle
  • Works with the Copper, Jade and Crystal Key runs
  • Catch: breaks on use, and its glow marks you for every enemy
05 · In-match challenges

Side quests mid-match

Four kinds of challenge spawn during every match. Each pays out on the spot, so detours are worth the risk.

Solo · Risky

Glitch Hunts

  • What: a flickering corrupted cube appears on the map
  • Clear it: stand inside and hold to "debug" for 15s while corrupted clones of nearby players attack you
  • Risk: a red static pillar shows your position to everyone, and corruption swaps your controls for a few seconds
  • Reward: an Epic weapon plus 1 Glitch Token for the Glitch Vault
Co-op · Social

Sector Events

  • What: a raid boss spawns in one sector: Ghost King (Midway), Ancient Dragon (Arcanum), Rogue AI Mech (Grid) or Block Titan (Bitlands)
  • Truce Dome: players inside can't hurt each other while the boss lives
  • After the kill: a 10s countdown, then the truce ends
  • Reward: every fighter gets a Legendary item. Top damage gets a Mythic roll. A "Raid Buddy" friend prompt pops up
Anyone · Quick

Mini Speed Challenges

  • What: small timed trials anyone can start: the neon car loop in 45s, an island parkour run, a grind-rail sprint or a block-jump climb
  • Beat the timer: a Rare item or a shield battery
  • Gold time: a Legendary item plus 2× XP for 5 minutes
  • Fastest in the match: your ghost replay haunts the course next match
Everyone vs #1

Bounty Protocols

  • What: the top real player on the match leaderboard gets a bounty marker
  • Hunters: the player who takes them out gets their bounty coins, a Legendary drop and 3× XP for the rest of the match
  • The target: survive 3 minutes with the bounty and earn a free shield battery and a Bounty Survivor badge
Safe Zones

Rest stops, with limits

  • No damage inside. Heal, sort inventory, chat and meet people
  • Suggested limit: 60s per visit, then a 2-minute cooldown
  • Safe Zones shut down when the Derez Wave reaches them
  • The bounty target can enter, but the 60s limit still applies

06 · Death & progression

What happens when you burst into coins

Three modes, from brutal to forgiving. Pick one to see what a player chooses on death.

Hardcore · no choice

Drop everything as a coin pile. Profile resets to Level 1.

Softened by

Hall of Fame entry, a legacy title ("Fallen Gunter – Lvl 87") and a small rebirth perk.

Best for

Seasonal "Ready Player One" event mode and ranked play.

Restart

Queue a fresh match. Cosmetics and account level are kept.

Continue

Respawn at the map edge with starter gear, once per match, not after Ring Phase 3. You glow as a "glitched" target.

Best for

New players (first ~10 hours) and the casual playlist.

Before the match

Insure 1–2 items with Glitch Tokens. Slot 1 is free. Insured items glow, so enemies know you carry something valuable.

Total Restart

Profile resets to Level 1, but your insured items come back to you.

Redemption Run

Continue as a blank Default Avatar. Reach the top 10 and you recover your full profile.

07 · Alternative win condition

The Easter Egg Hunt

Three gates, three keys, in strict order. You can't enter a gate without the key before it. Every player who beats a challenge earns that key, one per person.

Gate 1 · Copper Key

The Race

  • Beat elite NPC racers across all four sectors
  • Any NPC finishes first: no key
  • Other players can shoot you mid-race
  • Opens after Ring Phase 1
Gate 2 · Jade Key

The Tower

  • Climb a giant parkour tower full of traps
  • PvP allowed: knock rivals off
  • Key respawns 60s after each claim
  • Top platform crumbles after 10s
Gate 3 · Crystal Key

The Hunt

  • Find 10 hidden items in 5 minutes
  • Item spots randomise every attempt
  • Miss one: fail, cooldown, retry
  • Opens after Ring Phase 3

The Final Gate

Hold all three keys to open it. The first player through wins the match instantly. Last player standing still wins too.

Alerts

Only the first three are famous

  • 1st, 2nd and 3rd holders of each key trigger a global broadcast and land in every player's inbox
  • A key-coloured light beam shoots from the gate, visible map-wide
  • Music cuts to a 3-second chiptune fanfare
  • After the 3rd: no alerts, just a quiet counter on the Scoreboard
  • A Final Gate win always triggers an alert

08 · Character creator

Any body, same fair fight

A cyberpunk robot body with a wizard hat and 8-bit wings is fine. The look is free; the hitbox is fixed.

Fairness rules
  • One camera height so tall avatars can't peek over cover
  • Brightness floor and rim light so dark skins can't hide
  • Same footstep range for every body
  • Ranked lock option: standard outline on everyone
  • Hit effects follow the hitbox: a shot that only clips an oversized shoulder shows a miss spark
  • Certification test: every new cosmetic is checked against size, brightness and silhouette limits before release

Universal Emotes · step 1

Animate once

Every emote is built on one master skeleton, then auto-retargeted to each Core Rig.

Step 2

Per-body variants

A wave is an arm wave on a human, a paw in the air on a beast and a spin of particles on a floating orb.

Step 3

Style filter

Same emote, different timing: choppy 12fps for Pixel, servo stops for Robot, flowing trails for Fantasy.

Every emote picks up the style of the sector you're standing in. One animation file, four looks.

Player setting

Avatar, World or Blend

World first is the default. Avatar first keeps your outfit's style everywhere. Blend uses your animation with the world's effects.

Merge Zone

Style switches mid-move

Start a dance in the Grid, step into the Bitlands, and the second half plays in 8-bit.

Unlocks

World-exclusive emotes

Each sector hides 1–2 emotes, like "Insert Coin" in Midway or "Summon Familiar" in Arcanum. Once earned they work everywhere.

Fairness

Emote budget

Every emote has a fixed length and screen footprint, so no world's version lasts longer or blocks more view.

09 · Between matches

The hub, progression and the Gunter Pass

XP from matches and challenges raises your account level from 1 to 100. Every level unlocks something across three tracks. Unlocks add choice, not raw power, so new players can still win.

Track A

Chat Room customisation

  • Your own private room in the hub, starting as a bare basement
  • Unlock furniture, arcade cabinets, jukebox tracks and wall screens
  • Higher levels raise the room's guest limit, from 4 to 16
  • Trophy shelf shows your keys, raid kills and badges
Track B

Character upgrades

  • New Core Rigs, Body Frames, emotes and Motion Sets
  • Perk slots that let you choose a playstyle, such as faster healing or faster reload
  • Perks are sidegrades: picking one means giving up another
Track C

Weapon & item unlocks

  • Unlock new starting weapons and weapon skins
  • Unlocked starters are all equal in power to the default
  • Higher-tier loot is still found only in matches

Season Top 30

Finish the season in the top 30 of the leaderboard to earn perks for the next season. Below 30th, no perks. The Architect sits above the board and doesn't take a spot.
